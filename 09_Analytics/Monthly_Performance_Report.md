## 2. 7-DAY ITERATIVE REVIEW QUESTIONS

Every 7 days, the agency team answers:
1. Which carousel generated the highest save rate, and what technical topic did it cover?
2. Which Reel achieved >50% completion rate, and what hook was used in the first 2.5 seconds?
3. Did the 07:30 PM IST posting slot generate higher US founder non-follower reach than the 02:30 PM slot?
4. How many qualified B2B inquiries arrived via inbound DMs?
