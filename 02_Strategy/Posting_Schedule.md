# POSTING SCHEDULE & TIMEZONE OPTIMIZATION STRATEGY

**Primary Timezone:** Indian Standard Time (IST / UTC +5:30)  
**Target Markets:** India (Domestic) + US (East & West Coast) + UK / Europe  
**Reference Period:** November – December 2026  

---

## 1. THE TIMEZONE SYNCHRONIZATION MATRIX

Because Cypher Matrix markets both international B2B software/cybersecurity services and domestic Indian products, publishing times are divided into distinct behavioral windows:

| Window | Time (IST) | Equivalent Global Times | Target Audience & Content Type | Strategic Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Window 1: Domestic Morning** | **08:30 AM – 09:30 AM** | 03:00 AM GMT<br>10:00 PM EST (Prev Day) | **Indian SMBs & Agritech**<br>(Bill Mitra & Tractor Hisab) | Retail shop owners, traders, and fleet owners check phones while opening shops or before morning field runs. |
| **Window 2: European Morning / India Midday** | **02:00 PM – 03:30 PM** | 08:30 AM – 10:00 AM GMT<br>09:30 AM – 11:00 AM CET | **UK & European Tech Founders**<br>(Software Dev & Case Studies) | Catches European founders during morning commute/first coffee; catches Indian tech pros during post-lunch break. |
| **Window 3: US Morning / Global Prime** | **07:00 PM – 08:30 PM** | 08:30 AM – 10:00 AM EST<br>01:30 PM – 03:00 PM GMT | **US East Coast Founders & Global CTOs**<br>(Cybersecurity, VAPT, High-Depth Carousels) | **The #1 B2B Global Golden Hour:** US East Coast executives review industry feeds at start of workday; domestic Indian audience engages during evening leisure. |

## 3. 30-DAY POSTING-TIME TESTING METHODOLOGY

In adherence to scientific marketing standards, the proposed schedule represents our initial hypothesis. We will run an empirical 30-day posting-time test:

1. **Phase 1 (Days 1–10): Baseline Testing**
   - Publish strictly at the prescribed slot.
   - Record Reach, Impressions, Non-Follower %, and Initial 1-Hour Velocity.
2. **Phase 2 (Days 11–20): Slot Shifting (+/- 60 Minutes)**
   - Shift Window 3 from 07:30 PM to 08:30 PM IST.
   - Shift Window 2 from 02:30 PM to 03:30 PM IST.
   - Compare 1-hour engagement velocity and non-follower reach.
3. **Phase 3 (Days 21–30): Winner Consolidation**
   - Lock in the highest-performing 2-hour windows for each content category.
