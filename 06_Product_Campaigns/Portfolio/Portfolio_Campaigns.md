# PORTFOLIO & CASE STUDY MARKETING CAMPAIGNS

**Source:** Verified Deployments on [cyphermatrix.io](https://cyphermatrix.io/)  

---

## 1. GREEN LINE DISTRIBUTION (IFFCO Adblue)
- **Sector:** Agritech / Fleet Logistics.
- **Key Problem Solved:** Rural field connectivity drops.
- **Engineering Solution:** Offline-first SQLite local queues, background auto-sync, GPS route delivery logging.
- **Campaign Angle:** "Engineering apps that perform when 4G disappears."

## 2. VEGGIEKING RETAIL & LOGISTICS
- **Sector:** Perishable Grocery POS & Supply Chain.
- **Key Problem Solved:** High checkout latency and stock inventory loss.
- **Engineering Solution:** Sub-3-second POS, digital weighing scale integration, batch stock decrementation.
- **Campaign Angle:** "Zero-latency retail engineering for high-volume grocery."

## 3. GLAD AI & WORKSPACE SUITE
- **Sector:** Enterprise SaaS & AI Collaboration.
- **Key Problem Solved:** Real-time state synchronization across distributed teams.
- **Engineering Solution:** Low-latency WebSockets, RBAC token security, LLM workflow automation.
- **Campaign Angle:** "How we engineer real-time enterprise collaboration tools."

## 4. OPEN-SOURCE ODOO FLUTTER LIBRARY
- **Sector:** Developer Tooling & ERP Integration.
- **Key Problem Solved:** Slow and fragile ERP mobile integrations.
- **Engineering Solution:** High-speed RPC client with type-safe JSON serialization.
- **Campaign Angle:** "Why top engineering studios contribute back to open-source."
