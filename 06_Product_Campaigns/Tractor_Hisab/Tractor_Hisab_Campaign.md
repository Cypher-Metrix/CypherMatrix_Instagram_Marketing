# PRODUCT MARKETING CAMPAIGN: TRACTOR HISAB

**Product:** Tractor Hisab (Agritech Bookkeeping & Fleet Management)  
**Status:** Pre-Launch / Private Beta (Validating Problem Space & Waitlist)  
**Positioning:** Effortless hourly plowing (*jotai*) tracking, farmer credit ledger (*udhari*), diesel expenses, and driver attendance for rural tractor owners.  

---

## 1. CAMPAIGN STRATEGY & COMPLIANCE

In strict adherence to **Rule #4 ("Never invent product features")** and **Rule #12 ("Do not claim unverified downloads")**:
- Tractor Hisab is marketed strictly around **verified operational pain points** of agricultural machinery operators.
- No fictional app download numbers or fake client testimonials are used.
- All CTAs drive users to comment or DM 'TRACTOR' to join the **VIP Early Access Waitlist**.

---

## 2. CONTENT FOCUS AREAS

1. **Plowing Mathematics (*Jotai Ka Hisab*):** Tracking per-hour vs. per-bigha/acre operations accurately.
2. **Diesel & Expense Tracking:** Recording fuel purchases directly against machine working hours to detect leakage.
3. **Farmer Credit Ledger (*Kisan Udhari Khata*):** Eliminating lost paper diaries and disputes over pending balances.
4. **Driver Attendance & Advances:** Managing seasonal driver payouts and trip logs.
